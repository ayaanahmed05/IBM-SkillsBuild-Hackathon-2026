# 3-minute competition demo

## 0:00–0:18 — Hook

"Fans should not have to choose between experiencing the venue and experiencing the game. MomentGuard knows when to go and when to stay."

Show the opening critical-play screen: **STAY**, high Moment Risk, one-possession game.

## 0:18–0:50 — Explainable decision

Open the Moment Risk breakdown. Point out that the score is not an LLM guess or a probability. It is derived from validated game state. Turn on seat delivery and show that a high-risk game state produces a safer alternative instead of telling the fan to leave.

## 0:50–1:22 — Safe window and route

Use Judge mode → Timeout. The UI should change to **GO NOW** with a safe-window clock, trip requirement and safety margin. Select Restroom or Food. Show the highlighted SVG route and complete time breakdown. Start the smart route.

## 1:22–1:42 — Wow moment: recovery

Use Judge mode → Play resumed while the fan is away. The decision changes to **RETURN NOW** and Moment Recovery explains why. Emphasize that the system replans instead of assuming its original prediction was perfect.

## 1:42–2:05 — Accessibility

Reset, select a longer safe window, toggle Accessible routing and choose Fan shop. Explain that inaccessible graph edges/facilities are removed before routing, so a preference actually changes eligibility.

## 2:05–2:28 — Venue buyer story

Scroll to Venue Intelligence. Show current concourse pressure and the reproducible impact cards. State exactly: "These are simulated POC results, not real-world performance claims." Then highlight CrowdFlow's 100% → 50% peak food-concession concentration result.

## 2:28–2:48 — IBM architecture

Show the IBM-ready architecture panel. Explain the safety boundary: AI can orchestrate and explain, but deterministic tools validate the final action. Insert the genuine Bob and watsonx Orchestrate evidence captured by the team before submission.

## 2:48–3:00 — Close

"MomentGuard connects the game, the venue and the person in the seat. Know when to go. Stay for what matters."
